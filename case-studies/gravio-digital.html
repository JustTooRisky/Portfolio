Before

What I set out to build, and why a landing page alone wasn't enough

Gravio Digital is a Lagos-based agency that helps law firms convert inbound leads into paying clients. The brief was mine: build a business that sells web services to the same legal agencies I'm now applying to. A landing page alone wouldn't do that. The business needed a system: a way to identify firms, audit their sites, start conversations, and close engagements without a sales team.

  1. No pipeline. Without a systematic way to identify and contact target firms, outreach is random and unrepeatable.
  2. No audit method. "Your website needs work" isn't a conversation starter. The business needed a framework for evidence-based observations that justify professional outreach.
  3. No intake automation. A qualified lead hitting a generic contact form is revenue that disappears; the business needed a structured intake from day one.
  4. No proof. No live site to demonstrate capability. The landing page had to exist before any outreach could begin.

Approach

Build the system first, then the page

“I started with the storefront, then built the machine behind it so the offer could evolve from a general agency into a specialized legal-focused practice.”

The decision was to treat the business itself as the project. Every component had to earn its place:

  • A Dream 50 CRM tracking 50 target Lagos firms with contact details, observation notes, and call status.
  • A Python audit pipeline (Firecrawl + Playwright + vision LLM) that crawls each firm's site, captures screenshots, and produces scored observations (192 across 46 firms in the first run).
  • An observation pyramid ranking issues by wedge strength: contact & enquiry at the top, technical performance at the bottom, so every cold call starts with something the firm's decision-maker would actually care about.
  • A cold-call playbook built on consultative, observation-first principles (NEPQ-inspired, RPC Rule 39-conscious) designed to earn the next conversation, not close on the first call.
  • An investigation ladder with five thinking stages (ownership, process, awareness, evidence, future) that turn a cold call into a discovery conversation.
  • A fulfillment automation system for post-engagement delivery.

What runs today, end to end

Below is a distilled view of the system. The landing page converts. The pipeline identifies and audits. The playbook guides conversations. Each component is operational, not a mockup.

The landing page

graviodigital.com is a single-page sales mechanism: problem framing, offer stack (6 components + 2 bonuses), performance-based pricing (₦300k setup + ₦450k after 3 qualified leads), 90-day guarantee. Hand-coded HTML/CSS, no builder.

The audit pipeline

Python + Firecrawl + Playwright + Ollama (gemma4:31b). Maps each firm's site, selects relevant pages, captures screenshots, and produces scored JSON observations with wedge scores, evidence ladder placement, and conversation starters.

The cold-call playbook

Observation-first framework: one observation, one consequence, one question, one next step. 11 delivery principles. Designed for Nigerian law firms, RPC-conscious. Earns the next conversation, not the close.

The observation pyramid

10 categories ranked by wedge strength. Contact & enquiry and trust & credibility at the top; technical performance and tone at the bottom. Every observation scored on 5 criteria: factual, visible, relevant, explainable, creates curiosity.

Implementation

How each piece was built

The pipeline

  • Python orchestration (main.py) — runs the audit with resumable state (state.json)
  • Firecrawl/Crawl4AI for site mapping and markdown extraction
  • Playwright for multi-page screenshots
  • Ollama Cloud (gemma4:31b) for vision-based observation scoring
  • Chunked processing: images grouped in sets of 4 to stay within context limits

The playbook

  • V4 cold-call framework with 11 delivery principles
  • Four-point rule: one observation, one consequence, one question, one next step
  • Observation pyramid with wedge scoring (8–10 excellent, 6–7 good, 4–5 weak)
  • Investigation ladder: 5 thinking stages, not 5 mandatory questions
  • Rule 39 RPC-conscious language throughout

The landing page

  • Hand-coded HTML5, CSS custom properties, vanilla JS
  • Single accent green on ivory paper, Wittgenstein + Alegreya
  • Offer stack built with Alex Hormozi's value equation
  • Performance-based pricing: 90-day guarantee, results-triggered balance
