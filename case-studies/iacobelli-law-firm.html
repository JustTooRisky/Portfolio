Before

What was there, and what competed for the click

Reference: iacobellilawyers.com (captured Aug 2026). Iacobelli is a PI-exclusive firm with offices in Palm Beach Gardens FL (4440 PGA Blvd, Suite 600), Orlando FL, and Dallas / The Woodlands TX. Toll-free 866-234-6093, 24/7 answered. The homepage carries the right content but asks the injured visitor to do too much at once.

  1. Two competing forms. A “FREE CASE REVIEW” dropdown-heavy form in the hero plus a second “Nature of Accident / Type of Injury” form mid-page means the visitor must choose which one matters before they know what matters.
  2. Hero tries to do three jobs. “Your Road to Recovery Starts Here,” a generic firm intro, and an SMS-consent checkbox all sit above the fold; the most important action, talking to a lawyer now, is visually diluted.
  3. Navigation overload. 11 practice items and nested “Premises Liability > Slip and Fall / Trip and Fall” plus dual state pages (FL/TX) without a prioritised hierarchy means PI matters that should be scannable become a study task.
  4. Trust far from the CTA. “Million Dollar Advocates Forum” and contingency “No Win No Fee” signals appear well below the first form, not adjacent to the action they should reinforce.
  5. Form friction. A required “Type of Case” dropdown with 10 options, plus a separate SMS consent gate before submission, creates a high-abandonment pattern for injured users on mobile.

Approach

I wanted to make one thing obvious

“I wanted to make the next step unmissable, reduce the choice burden, and put trust beside the action, not three scrolls below it.”

The redesign treats the homepage as a triage page, not a catalogue.

  • One hero, one form: three fields (name, phone, and case type with 5 plain-English options), one action (“Get my free case review”), and a trust signal (“No win, no fee · 24/7 answered”) directly beside the button.
  • Collapse 11 practice links into four urgency-weighted groups: Vehicle (car, truck, motorcycle), Premises & Product, Medical & Nursing Home, and Catastrophic & Wrongful Death. Each links to a focused intake, not a generic listing.
  • Move “Million Dollar Advocates” and “Millions recovered” adjacent to the hero CTA, not buried mid-page; proximity converts.
  • Dual-state clarity: one location row (Palm Beach Gardens / Orlando / Dallas / The Woodlands) with toll-free 866-234-6093 and call-to-call tel: links on every CTA, with no duplicate FL/TX nav.
  • Shorten the friction chain: remove the SMS gate from the critical path (collect consent post-intake), shorten dropdowns, and add an inline “Prefer to call?” fallback for low-form-comfort visitors.

Implementation

How it was built

Conversion-first layout

  • Hero grid 1.15fr / 0.85fr collapses to a single column at 820px; the form stays adjacent to trust on desktop and stacks below on mobile.
  • Single CTA hierarchy: “Call” and “Get my free case review,” with no competing second form.
  • All tel links use href="tel:…" for click-to-call.

Reduced friction

  • 3 fields vs 6+ on the original; 5 plain-English case options vs 10+.
  • SMS consent removed from the critical path and collected post-intake to reduce abandonment.
  • Inline fallback “Prefer to call?” for visitors who won’t complete a form when injured.

Semantic & accessible

  • Form uses associated label / input / select with required and visible focus.
  • Trust badges are text with border, not image-only; readable without alt concerns.
  • Contrast: green CTA #145C46 on paper #F4F1EA passes AA for button text; ink on paper for body.

Honest mapping

  • Offices and toll-free (4440 PGA Blvd, 866-234-6093) are the firm’s public details.
  • Practice clusters map to the firm’s listed areas: vehicle, premises, boating, malpractice, catastrophic, and wrongful death.
  • No invented verdicts: “Millions recovered” references the firm’s stated results without a specific fabricated amount.
