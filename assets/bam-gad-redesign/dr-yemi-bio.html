Overview & Biography

Dr. Yemi Opemuti is a distinguished Nigerian attorney, commercial law strategist, and international business solicitor with over two decades of unbroken corporate, commercial, and civil practice. As Lead Partner at Bam & Gad Solicitors, he drives the firm’s vision for legal excellence, client satisfaction, and innovative problem-solving.

Admitted to the Nigerian Bar in 2000, he has built a reputation as a foremost authority in Foreign Direct Investment, cross-border trade facilitation, corporate governance, intellectual property protection, and high-stakes commercial litigation — appearing across the High Courts, Court of Appeal, and the Supreme Court of Nigeria.

His career spans leading commercial practices in Ikoyi and Ikeja, including Tuyi, Komaya & Co. (Partner and Head of Commercial Practice) and Tunde, Oladumuye, Gabriel & Co. His counsel has facilitated multi-million dollar foreign investments into Nigerian sub-sectors, guiding international brands through regulatory compliance, joint ventures, and market entry.

FDI & International Trade

Unlocking Nigerian and African markets for foreign capital and global enterprises — structuring cross-border deals and guiding investors through the Nigerian regulatory framework.

  • Market entry & incorporation: foreign-owned subsidiaries, branch offices, and joint ventures under NIPC guidelines.
  • Capital importation & FX compliance: Certificates of Capital Importation (CCI), dividend repatriation, CBN monetary regulations.
  • Licensing & permits: Business Permits, Expatriate Quotas, CERPAC, and sector licences (SEC, NCC, NAFDAC, DPR/NUPRC).
  • Cross-border M&A and JVs: shareholder agreements, asset purchase agreements, foreign equity participation structures.
